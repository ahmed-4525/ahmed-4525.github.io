01 / ABOUT

Research grounded in practical impact.

I am an aquaculture researcher from Bangladesh with academic training in fisheries and aquaculture, and a strong interest in developing sustainable, resource-efficient approaches to aquatic food production.

My research experience spans aquafeed formulation, fish nutrition, growth performance, feed quality, mineral utilization and aquatic animal health. I am particularly interested in transforming locally available biological by-products into functional ingredients for more sustainable aquaculture feeds.

Through laboratory and feeding-trial research, I have worked with Nile tilapia, brown shrimp and indigenous freshwater fish. I aim to expand this foundation through advanced research in fish physiology, molecular diagnostics and precision nutrition.

03 / SELECTED WORK

Research projects

Selected themes from my research and experimental experience.

01

AQUACULTURE NUTRITION · NILE TILAPIA

Shell-derived minerals as aquafeed ingredients

Investigated shell powders from Pomacea maculata, Viviparus viviparus and Mytilus edulis as alternative mineral sources in Nile tilapia diets, with attention to feed quality, mineral composition and fish performance.

Mineral nutritionFeed qualityICP-OES
02

SHRIMP NUTRITION · BROWN SHRIMP

Formulated diets for Metapenaeus monoceros

Contributed to diet formulation and feeding-trial research evaluating growth, feed utilization, water stability and economic performance of formulated diets for brown shrimp.

Feed formulationGrowth performanceEconomic indices
03

AQUATIC FOOD QUALITY · ROHU

Egg quality of wild and cultured Rui

Worked on comparative assessment of egg nutritional composition, fatty-acid profiles and sensory characteristics of Rui (Labeo rohita) from haor systems and aquaculture settings.

Fatty-acid profileFish reproductionHaor ecosystems
04

ALTERNATIVE LIPID SOURCES · CLIMBING PERCH

Black soldier fly larvae oil in aquafeeds

Undergraduate research experience involving feeding trials, feed preparation and water-quality monitoring to explore insect-derived oil as an alternative lipid source in climbing perch diets.
